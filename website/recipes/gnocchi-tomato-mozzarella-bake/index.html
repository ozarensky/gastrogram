Gnocchi, tomato and mozzarella bake

25 min total8 min hands-on2 adults, 2 toddlersMediterranean

Last day for the basil.

ToddlersTheirs comes from the middle of the dish where it is cooler and milder — leave the chilli flakes off that half entirely. Halve the gnocchi for the 2-year-old.

Ingredients7 items

  • 800 gfresh gnocchi
  • 2chopped tomatoes
  • 250 gmozzarella
  • 1 bunchfresh basil
  • 1red onion
  • 1garlic
  • 100 gparmesan

Method5 steps

  1. Oven to 220C.
  2. Soften sliced red onion and garlic 4 minutes, add both tins of tomatoes, oregano and a pinch of sugar. Bubble 5 minutes.
  3. Stir the raw gnocchi straight into the sauce — no need to boil it first.
  4. Tip into a baking dish. Tear mozzarella over, grate parmesan on top. Chilli flakes on one half only.
  5. Bake 15 minutes until blistered. Basil over, stand 3 minutes before serving — it comes out molten.
